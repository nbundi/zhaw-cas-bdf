## Changes versus FS 2025

- The CAS now runs **Blockchain** as Module 1 (FS 2025: *DAOs & Metaverse*), with a new
  *Blockchain & AI* slot and Legal Aspects folded into the DAOs day.
- **DeFi Custody** as a standalone session is gone; wallets move into the combined
  *Wallets & Payments* online block, now shared with a forensics segment.
- **Decentralized Derivatives** moves from the MEV/Regulation day into the online
  asset-management block; the 04.12. day is therefore MEV + Regulation only.
- *Privacy, Zero-Knowledge Proofs and Compliance* is now firmly scheduled (20.11.)
  rather than listed without a date.
